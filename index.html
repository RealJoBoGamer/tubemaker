    How to build your map

    Getting started

    • New ▸ TfL template loads the London network (Tube, DLR, Elizabeth line and London Overground — Thameslink, Cable Car and Trams are left out).
    • New ▸ Blank map starts from scratch.
    • Your work autosaves in this browser. Use Save map file to keep a copy.

    Lines

    • + Add line: pick a TfL line, a community preset, or make a custom line with any colour and style.
    • Click a line in the list to make it active, then use Draw line: click stations in order (click empty space to create a new station). Press Esc to finish a branch.
    • Clicking a station that is the end of the active line continues that branch.

    Editing

    • Select: drag stations, labels, symbols, text, the key, zone corners and river points. Drag the background to pan, scroll to zoom.
    • Tracks always run horizontal, vertical or at 45°: new stations snap to 45° from the previous one (hold Alt to place freely) and dragged stations snap into line with their neighbours. Click a segment to flip its bend or make it straight; Map settings ▸ Straighten whole map tidies any map.
    • Station panel: rename, move label (8 positions), step-free access, National Rail, airport, river, coach and closed flags.
    • Connector: click two stations to join them with an interchange “blob” (internal) or a dotted walking link.
    • Symbol: stamp any key symbol anywhere on the map.

    Shortcuts

    V select · S station · L line · C connector · K symbol · T text · E erase · Del delete · arrows nudge · Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Ctrl+S save file
